## 1.1.0 (2026-09-30)
The first public release. ARISE was built privately through 0.1 and 0.2. This version collects all of that work.

### Challenges
- **75 Hard, 75 Soft or Custom.** Custom lets you pick from 12 preset tasks or add your own, choose 30–100 days, and decide whether a miss restarts it
- **Missed days (75 Hard):** restart from Day 1, or say "I did finish it" with one of **3 free passes per attempt**. The sheet counts them down, and once they're gone a restart is the only option
- **Pause:** name a break and choose 1–60 days. While you're away, Home shows how long is left and a nudge to eat mindfully and keep moving, and there are no reminders or missed-day sheets. You get one "welcome back" notification when it ends, then restart at Day 1 or pause longer
- Past attempts, a best streak, and milestones (Week 1, Week 2, Halfway, Final stretch, Done)

### Home and Week
- A Today ring with one arc per task. The card turns to the accent colour when every task is done, with confetti and haptics
- **Tasks with a bit of help:**
  - Workout sheet (type, minutes, outdoors, 45-minute rule)
  - Read sheet (book, page, total)
  - Meditate sheet (opens your meditation app, with a built-in timer)
  - The progress photo ticks itself once there's a photo for the day
- Week: a task × day grid with a score per task, workout minutes, pages read and a day total

### Photos
- Camera or gallery, with front, side and back angles, grouped by the week of each photo's own attempt
- Before/after slider. The before photo can be pinned, and it stays pinned through restarts

### Health Connect (read-only)
- Weight, height, workouts, sleep, steps and mindfulness sessions
- Today's workout sessions appear as picks in the Workout sheet (never ticked automatically). The sleep panel shows a 7-night chart. Day 1's sleep is free

### Profile & settings
- Name and photo, challenge stats, body (height, weight, BMI, goal), and a reading log with books finished
- Appearance: light, dark or system; 7 accent colours; dot-matrix or mono numbers; confetti on or off
- An evening reminder, sent only if tasks are still open
- Backup and restore of everything, photos included, in one zip

### Food (optional)
- Connect your own [arise-food](https://github.com/minimal-designer/arise-food) server to see calories, macros, meals and weigh-ins. Without one, the Food tab and calories card stay hidden, and Home shows your best streak instead
